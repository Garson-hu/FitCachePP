2026-06-14 TPDS revision note. The GNN warm-speedup cells below (18.6–33.2×) were found to be regime-contaminated by the review panel (the warm cell is partly page-cache served and the native baseline was the most favorable Lustre condition, mislabeled “cold”). They are superseded by a regime-pinned rerun: NVMe-bound warm 82.7 s, page-cache resident ~5 s, cold native 1870–2457 s → ~6× vs favorable Lustre, 22–30× vs cold. A 32–256-node Megatron warm-flat ladder (175–178 s flat) and a DRAM-tier (tmpfs) warm-hit proof were also added. See EXPERIMENTS_STATUS.html § TPDS Revision Campaign for the authoritative tables; the raw cells below are kept as the record of the original C1 sweep.
