The newer real-case layout (8 DataLoader ranks/node — experiments.html §1.0) supersedes several: Megatron true-cold anchor is now 30.2× @16 GPUs (not 5.5×); Vision 5.2×/4.0× @16/32 GPUs; Vision N≥8 and the exact-footprint values are pending a warm-fallback/re-promote fix. Read-only audit: every quantitative experimental claim in Sections/*.tex + Figs/5-Extension/make_extension_figs.py was extracted and cross-checked against numbers re-derived from the raw logs (via the map files under benchmarks/results/frontier/, not the summary reports). The paper has not been edited. Base-paper (IPDPS) numbers are kept by policy and were excluded.

Overall. The extension's qualitative claims survive and are mostly strengthened by the re-measurement: the 1×-vs-N× dichotomy is now backed by a textbook-clean measured 1/N curve (Megatron 515G→4.0G/node, Vision 1.0T→8.0G/node, through 2048 GPUs) and GNN's flat 841 GiB N×. But essentially every quantitative extension number in the paper predates the campaign: the 20.6× headline is stale, all 24 cells of tab:mmap_speedup are wrong, the Megatron 512-GPU cap inverts (it now reaches 2048), and DINOv2 no longer exists as a workload.
